                How to read the graph

                • Done: a decision someone authorized, or a finding whose evidence was recorded (not proof it is true)
                • Ready: can be worked on now
                • Parked: waiting on something earlier
                • Deferred: set aside on purpose
                • Superseded: replaced (dashed)
                • Rounded box: a decision, settled by who chose it
                • Pill: a finding, supported by evidence within a stated scope
                • Purple outline: the question being asked now
                • →Solid arrow A → B: B waits for A (workflow order only; it does not mean A proves B)
                • ⇢Dashed arrow A ⇢ B: A is evidence for B (drawn for the selected node only)
                • Observedseen directly, within its scope
                • Inferredconcluded from observations, with limits
                • Not establishedthis investigation has not found out; not the same as "does not exist"
                • Unclassified recordrecorded before findings were typed

                The goal is not drawn; every item serves it. Click a node for its answer, authorization, evidence and history. "Needs review" means evidence behind a node changed; its answer and authorization are untouched.
